# Module 54 content contract

Preserve the Amyloid-Beta fibril (`2BEG`) and `KLVFF` capstone context while
distinguishing a preparation outline from execution-qualified evidence. Do not
claim a validated pose, complete preparation, simulation readiness, inhibition
or biological effect from the bounded geometry tests.

The geometric characterization must retain:

- Explicit target/candidate atom-index populations, their merge-order source
  correspondence, nonempty/disjoint/unique admission and remapping after edits.
- Extraction of the dry pair and each isolated partner at the same coordinates.
  Output selection alone does not remove SASA occluders.
- Native Shrake–Rupley sampling, identical radii/probe/sampling choices in all
  three calculations, explicit Cartesian geometry and whole/common-image inputs.
- Separate total SASA loss and half-area convention, quantity-bearing arithmetic,
  explicit output units and the original method/convention references.
- Criterion-specific H-bond counts with explicit-H inventory limits; no stability,
  affinity, free-energy or equilibration conclusion from either observable.

Rose/Janin reference descriptors must never substitute for geometric interface
measurements. Keep full biological preparation qualification separate from
bounded analytic execution of the actual characterization cell. Do not publish
synthetic outputs under the biological project's labels.
