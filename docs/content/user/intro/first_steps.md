# First steps

This page is a gentle bridge between the very first contact with MolSysMT and
the more detailed conceptual material in the Introduction.

If you have just installed MolSysMT, we recommend:

1. **Running the Quickstart guide**  
   Visit the Quickstart tutorial in the Showcase section:

   - [Quickstart guide](../../showcase/quickstart.ipynb)

   This notebook walks you through loading a system, inspecting basic
   information, and performing a few simple operations. It is the fastest way
   to get a feeling for the library.

2. **Exploring a couple of showcase examples**  
   After the Quickstart, you can browse other Showcase notebooks to see what
   MolSysMT can do in realistic scenarios (system preparation, trajectory
   analysis, working with OpenMM or NGLView, etc.).

3. **Looking at Cookbook recipes**  
   The [Cookbook](../cookbook/index.md) collects user-oriented recipes that
   combine several tools to solve specific tasks. Once you are comfortable with
   the Quickstart, skimming one or two recipes can help you see how the pieces
   fit together in practice.

After you have completed at least the Quickstart and perhaps one additional
example or recipe, you will have enough context to benefit much more from the
rest of the Introduction pages (Molecular systems, Native forms, Selection
language, and so on).*** End Patch ***!
