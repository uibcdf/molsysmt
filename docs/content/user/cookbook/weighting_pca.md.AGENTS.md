# Weighted PCA recipe

Preserve Cookbook_Weighting_PCA and the bundled-particle workflow. Keep atom
weight order, repeated structure observations, Cartesian feature blocks,
square-root weighting, population covariance and row-eigenvector projection
checks explicit. Do not normalize weights by their sum or compare arbitrary
degenerate eigenvectors component by component. Retain squared-length units,
finite positive-sum weight validation and the dense-memory boundary.
