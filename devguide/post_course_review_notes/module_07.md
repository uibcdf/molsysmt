# Post-Review Notes: Module 7 (Discovery & Capabilities)

These points were identified during the Master review on April 21, 2026.

---

### 1. Conceptual Rigor
- **Attribute Definition:** Avoid calling them just "consultable properties". Define them as **"Molecular System Properties"** that represent the identity and state of the system, which can be both queried (`get`) and modified (`set`).
- **Attribute Taxonomy:** Ensure the distinction between **Topological** (static identity), **Structural** (geometric state), and **Physical** (intrinsic or calculated properties) attributes is consistently used.

### 2. Pedagogy
- **Bidirectionality:** Reinforce that discovery functions (`has_attribute`) tell you not only what you can see, but what you can potentially change in the system.
