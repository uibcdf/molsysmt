    "import molsysmt as msm\n",
    "from molsysmt import systems\n",
    "\n",
    "# Load our solvated complex\n",
    "fibril = msm.convert('pdb:2BEG', to_form='molsysmt.MolSys')\n",
    "peptide = msm.build.build_peptide('KLVFF')\n",
    "molsys = msm.merge([fibril, peptide])"
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "markdown",
   "metadata": {},
   "source": [
    "### 1. Mass and Charge\n",
    "Every Master needs to know the scale of their system. Is it a small peptide or a massive polymer?"
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "# Calculate total mass and total charge of the whole system\n",
    "mass = msm.physchem.get_mass(molsys)\n",
    "charge = msm.physchem.get_charge(molsys)\n",
    "\n",
    "print(f\"Total Mass: {mass}\")\n",
    "print(f\"Total Net Charge: {charge}\")"
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "markdown",
   "metadata": {},
   "source": [
    "### 2. Solvent Accessible Surface Area (SASA)\n",
    "SASA measures how much of the molecule is \"visible\" to the water molecules. It is a proxy for solubility and folding stability."
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "# Calculate SASA for the peptide alone\n",
    "sasa_peptide = msm.physchem.get_sasa(molsys, selection='molecule_type==\"peptide\"')\n",
    "\n",
    "print(f\"Peptide SASA: {sasa_peptide}\")"
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "markdown",
   "metadata": {},
   "source": [
    "### 3. Interface Analysis: Buried Area\n",
    "This is the \"Gold Standard\" for binding analysis. The **Buried Area** is the surface area that is lost when two molecules come together. A higher buried area usually means a stronger binding."
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "code",
   "execution_count": null,
   "metadata": {},
   "outputs": [],
   "source": [
    "# Calculate area buried between the peptide and the fibril\n",
    "buried_area = msm.physchem.get_area_buried(molsys, selection='molecule_type==\"peptide\"', \n",
    "                                           selection_2='molecule_type==\"protein\"')\n",
    "\n",
    "print(f\"Total Buried Area: {buried_area}\")\n",
    "\n",
    "# You can also get the fraction of the peptide that is buried\n",
    "fraction = msm.physchem.get_buried_fraction(molsys, selection='molecule_type==\"peptide\"', \n",
    "                                            selection_2='molecule_type==\"protein\"')\n",
    "\n",
    "print(f\"Fraction of peptide surface hidden in the complex: {fraction*100:.1f}%\")"
   ]
