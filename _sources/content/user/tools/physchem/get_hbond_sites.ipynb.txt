    "## Choosing chemical definitions\n",
    "\n",
    "| Method | Donor element bonded to indexed H | Acceptor rule |\n",
    "| --- | --- | --- |\n",

    "| elemental_fluorine_oxygen_nitrogen | F/O/N | Every F/O/N |\n",
