    "## Summary of dihedral angle definitions\n",
    "\n",
    "The tables below summarize the standard atom quartet definitions used by MolSysMT:\n",
    "\n",
    "| Angle | Atoms Definition | Standard Range |\n",
    "| :---: | :---: | :---: |\n",
    "| $\\\\phi$ | $C_{i-1} - N_i - C\\\\alpha_i - C_i$ | $[-180^\\\\circ, 180^\\\\circ]$ |\n",
    "| $\\\\psi$ | $N_i - C\\\\alpha_i - C_i - N_{i+1}$ | $[-180^\\\\circ, 180^\\\\circ]$ |\n",
    "| $\\\\omega$ | $C\\\\alpha_i - C_i - N_{i+1} - C\\\\alpha_{i+1}$ | $[-180^\\\\circ, 180^\\\\circ]$ |\n",
    "| $\\\\chi_1$ | $N_i - C\\\\alpha_i - C\\\\beta_i - C\\\\gamma_i$ | $[-180^\\\\circ, 180^\\\\circ]$ |\n"
   ]
