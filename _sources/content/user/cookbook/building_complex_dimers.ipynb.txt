    "(Cookbook_Building_Complex_Dimers)=\n",
    "# Building Complex Dimers\n",
    "\n",
    "*Harvesting, aligning, and merging monomers from disparate PDB structures into a complete complex.*\n",
    "\n",
    "Biological assemblies deposited in the Protein Data Bank often present incomplete asymmetric units, missing loops, or alternate conformations split across different chains.
